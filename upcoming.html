CURATOR Planning Announcement

Planned Changes for Next Release

What We're Working On

These are the improvements currently planned for the next CURATOR release. Development is still underway, so the final list may change before release.

Adding

  • Adding support for decoding CDI, NRG, and MDF/MDS images through bundled Aaru libraries while preserving original sources.
  • Adding content-authoritative Redump CUE resolution for supported optical sets.
  • Adding exact collector-facing routing for Sega CD 32X sets without changing DAT platform authority.
  • Adding a Stage 10 output-wide receipt manifest and deterministic Append verification.

Changing

  • Changing Stage 9 to index execution-contract destinations once while preserving collision handling and exact downstream instruction order.
  • Changing Stage 8 to resolve previously selected exact shared physical sources through a per-set keyed lookup while preserving grouping, selection, and output order.
  • Changing Stage 1 to use Core-owned bounded DAT leading-text reads and time-throttled progress rendering while preserving exact platform authority.
  • Changing Stage 4 to construct its ordered inventory and summary accounting in one pass while retaining Core-owned enumeration and publication.
  • Changing Stage 2 to reuse each DAT's fail-closed platform association instead of replaying a second verification and progress-render pass.
  • Changing Stage 5 to capture Size, CRC32, and SHA-1 while archive members are extracted and loose files are copied, reuse persisted stream evidence while retaining Core-owned fallback rereads, skip redundant filesystem queries for complete immutable prepared identities, and check archive recovery residue once per resumed run.
  • Changing Stage 5 to perform bounded parallel CHD extraction.
  • Changing Stage 6 to reuse prepared hashes, report checked, reused, normalization-read, and special-disc identity counts accurately to the end user, preserve Stage 7 compatibility, and resolve optical authority strictly from content.
  • Changing Stage 6 to trust the completed Stage 5 prepared-file contract, remove its redundant existence-only preflight, reuse immutable matching evidence, and reduce repeated output-record construction.
  • Changing Stage 7 to validate immutable upstream identities without rehashing files and to resolve DAR member evidence through direct keyed lookups while preserving exact validation output, including a dedicated contextual lookup only for records that require contextual CUE authority.
  • Changing Stage 3 to use cached and Core-owned batch identity hashes, batched comparison serialization, indexed reconciliation, and guarded company-summary reuse.
  • Changing Stage 9 to publish the complete physical-output execution contract and Stage 10 to consume it directly.
  • Changing TaskBars to use deterministic numeric progress and suppress stale or regressive frames.
  • Changing the Core API handbook to document 149 interfaces.

Fixing

  • Fixing publication sets whose distinct authority entry names intentionally share exact physical content, while retaining strict non-publication and optical membership handling.
  • Fixing duplicate sources satisfying multiple optical memberships.
  • Fixing ZIP- and loose-origin CUE repair.
  • Fixing filename-based CHD authority scoring.
  • Fixing configured non-set files (.txt, .exe, .dll, and .bat) entering Needs Attention output.
  • Fixing authoritative Jaguar CD CUE handling.
  • Fixing Gateway folder-path presentation.
  • Fixing Core build identities and stale project documentation.

Continuing Limitations

  • Continuing investigation of Reporting classifying the root receipt manifest as unmanifested content (DEFERRED-053).
  • Continuing the deferred Stage 3 and Stage 5 progress-presentation trace audit (DEFERRED-055).
  • Continuing Stage 6 DAR-index and large-output construction/publication investigation (DEFERRED-045 and DEFERRED-057).
