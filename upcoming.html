CURATOR Planning Announcement

Planned Changes for Next Release

What We're Working On

These are the improvements currently planned for the next CURATOR release. Development is still underway, so the final list may change before release.

Adding

  • Adding content-validated MAME -listxml ingestion for Arcade ROM and CHD authority.

Changing

  • Changing MAME Arcade output to split-set structure with clone-owned ROM membership and separate dependency sets.
  • Prioritizing MAME internal CHD SHA-1 authority over Redump-derived optical-track evidence.
  • Changing complete authoritative-set publication so applicable optical transformation requires one CUE while complete sets without usable CUE evidence publish as ZIP sets.
  • Preserving distinct Redump and TOSEC authority sets when identical content appears in both sources.
  • Selecting Redump cue authority by authoritative DAT SHA-256 and pinning each configured cue archive by SHA-256.
  • Refreshing the bundled Redump cue and GDI metadata archives and expanding configured platform coverage.

Fixing

  • Fixing resolved candidate destination collisions remaining as blocking readiness failures after final artifact routing produces distinct destinations.
  • Retaining existing authority memberships when optical-container refinement adds deterministic set-scoped evidence.
  • Collapsing exact duplicate records within one authority membership before DAR publication while preserving distinct Redump and TOSEC memberships.
