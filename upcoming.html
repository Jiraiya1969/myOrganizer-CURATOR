Upcoming Changes

Updated On: August 26, 2026 at 7:30 PM EDT

What We're Working On

These are the improvements currently planned for the next CURATOR release. Development is still underway, so the final list may change before release.

Changing

  • Changing deterministic matching to test the prepared raw size, CRC32, and SHA-1 identity first and perform content-structural normalization only after an exact raw authority miss, without filename or extension selection.
  • Separating NeedsAttention review output into cartridge, disk-image, optical-media, and unresolved-media branches using authoritative platform media categories without filename or extension inference.
  • Presenting Matching phase, refinement-subset, and final totals with distinct end-user labels that agree with the authoritative completion summary.
  • Presenting Assembly processing in end-user language without exposing its internal database implementation.
  • Changing Stage 10 frozen-plan loading and permanent collection publication to bounded, set-based database operations so Organizer remains an output stage at collection scale.

Fixing

  • Honoring caller-supplied Core JSON progress labels and reporting collection manifest serialization against its exact record count.
  • Announcing successful completion of Organizer recovery safeguards before physical output begins when no determinate taskbar is available.
  • Starting Organizer physical-output progress only when physical writing begins, with the correct resumed-output count and output-specific status.
  • Announcing the start and completion of permanent collection commit and current-manifest loading as distinct Stage 10 operations.
  • Preventing a completed Stage 9 NO_GO result from continuing into Stage 10, while preserving complete Stage 9 publication, reporting, and cleanup.
  • Blocking a direct Stage 10 launch against a Stage 9 NO_GO receipt before destination clearing or physical output begins.
  • Replacing Windows-invalid filename characters in the CURATOR A8P originals DAT without changing its content identity metadata.
