Upcoming Changes

Updated On: August 28, 2026 at 9:46 AM EDT

What We're Working On

These are the improvements currently planned for the next CURATOR release. Development is still underway, so the final list may change before release.

Adding

  • Adding direct HyperList menu XML ingestion with complete catalog retention and deterministic CRC identity enrichment.
  • Adding the CURATOR-created Atari ST Atarimania GameBase authority DAT to the required application authority set.

Fixing

  • Fixing direct CHD handling so already-valid source CHDs are preserved by copy instead of being routed through CUE-led reconstruction.
  • Fixing menu-driven Stage 9 NO-GO handling so the Gateway exits directly to PowerShell, and restoring the Organize preset's final Stage 10 pre-summary acknowledgement without adding pauses between earlier stages or after the Stage 10 summary.
  • Fixing Atari 2600 HyperList authority names to use game/@name instead of description text containing Windows-invalid characters.
  • Fixing successfully traversed raw-unmatched embedded archives so their containers do not become duplicate NeedsAttention outputs while extracted descendants retain independent matching and review decisions.
