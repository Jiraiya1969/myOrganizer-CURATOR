CURATOR Planning Announcement

Planned Changes for a Future Release

What We're Working On

v2.0.0-beta.1 is the current public release. These are possible changes after that release. They are not live, released, promised, or guaranteed, and inclusion here is not evidence that an item is completed or approved for a future release.

Adding

  • Adding a verified supplemental Atari Lynx Dragnet DAT with Fujiology, independent-header, and headerless identities.
  • Adding database-backed permanent collection manifests and retained reporting history.
  • Adding explicit Standard, Full Audit, Reconcile, and Detailed Export reporting actions.
  • Adding Reporting console announcements for new permanent collection items in the latest Organizer generation, including a platform breakdown.

Changing

  • Changing cartridge-header normalization to support signatures at explicit byte offsets.
  • Changing Reporting to derive current collection results from the permanent database record.
  • Changing Stage 10 and Reporting console messages to explain long-running work clearly.

Fixing

  • Fixing Atari Lynx and Atari 7800 matching by validating their platform-specific headers before removing them.
  • Fixing silent console periods during output preparation, manifest publication, and reporting reconciliation.
  • Fixing Stage 5 database publication for CUE descriptors whose referenced files are absent while retaining strict foreign-key protection for bound references.
  • Fixing Stage 5 loose-file SHA-1 identities by initializing incremental copy-hash providers before processing bytes.
  • Fixing zero-new-item collection manifests so Organizer and Reporting return an empty platform breakdown without failing.
